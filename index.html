About us

Welcome to tutrSTEM. - Specialist STEM, Economics & Psychology Tutoring

At tutrSTEM., we specialise in subject mastery and university admissions preparation for GCSE and A-Level candidates. Working with tutors from top-tier institutions, including Cambridge and Imperial, we deliver focused instruction across STEM subjects, Economics, and Psychology.

Our MissionWe make rigorous, subject-specific academic support accessible and effective by matching ambitious candidates with tutors who have mastered the qualifications and entrance assessments they are working toward.
Top-Tier TutorsOur network includes tutors from leading universities like Cambridge and Imperial with deep expertise in their disciplines.
Tailored MentorshipStructured guidance built around exam board specifications, learning style, and academic targets.
Specialized University AdmissionsDedicated coaching for competitive university entrance tests and challenging academic tracks.
Flexible LearningSeamless online and local sessions structured around your timetable and revision schedule.
Academic SubjectsGCSE and A-Level Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Statistics, Mechanics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Psychology, Computer Science, programming, logic, and computer systems.
Admissions PreparationSupport for ESAT, TMUA, MAT, STEP, Oxbridge assessments, and targeted AQA, Edexcel, OCR, and Cambridge International preparation.
Our StorytutrSTEM. was created to meet the growing demand for specialised, subject-focused support across STEM, Economics, and Psychology, helping learners build the analytical skills, subject knowledge, and exam strategies needed for competitive GCSEs, A-Levels, and university entrance tests.
